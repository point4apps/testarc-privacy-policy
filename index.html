TestArc Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Point4 ("we", "our", or "us") provides TestArc, an exam preparation and mock-test application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

1. Information We May Handle

  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, authentication information made available through Google Firebase Authentication may be processed, such as your account identifier, email address, and profile name.
  • Learning activity: Test attempts, answers, scores, and progress may be stored or processed to provide app features.
  • Question reports: If you submit a question report, the information you enter and associated account information may be processed to review the report.
  • Technical information: Google or Firebase services may process technical information as described in their own privacy documentation.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide sign-in, deliver exam and revision features, maintain test progress, review question reports, secure the service, and troubleshoot problems.

3. Third-Party Services

TestArc may use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Firestore. These services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

4. Data Storage and Security

Information required for app features may be stored locally on your device or using Firebase services, depending on the feature. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide app features, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations. For questions or requests concerning your information, contact us using the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

TestArc is an exam preparation app. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary for the app's intended functions. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

7. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use Google Sign-In. Some features may not work without an account. You can contact us with questions about access to or deletion of information associated with your account.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app or practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
