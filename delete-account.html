TestArc Account and Data Deletion

Users of TestArc can request deletion of their account and associated personal data without reinstalling the app.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to our support address from the email address associated with your TestArc account. Use the subject line TestArc Account Deletion Request.

Request Account Deletion by Email

In your request, identify the Google account used to sign in to TestArc. Please do not send your password or any authentication codes.

What Data May Be Deleted

  • Your TestArc account associated with Firebase Authentication.
  • Question feedback reports associated with your account, where applicable.
  • Other personal data associated with your account that we control and are required to delete.

What May Remain on Your Device

Test attempts, scores, bookmarks, and other learning progress may be stored locally on your device. Account deletion may not automatically remove locally stored app data. You may need to clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove that local data.

Request Verification and Processing

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account before processing a deletion request. We will respond about the request and explain if any data must be retained for a legal or security reason.
